# PORTAL 001 | BEYOND REALITY
### An Immersive 3D Interdimensional Portal Experience

Built with React, Vite, TypeScript, Three.js, React Three Fiber, @react-three/drei, and custom procedural GLSL Shaders.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Scene 1: The Mysterious Ancient Room**
   - High-fidelity stone architecture with damp, reflective flagstones.
   - Massive 3D circular portal structure with 12 engraved runes and central energy vortex.
   - Atmospheric volumetric lighting and dust particles.

2. **Scene 2: Dynamic Portal Activation & Custom GLSL Shaders**
   - Interactive hold-to-charge mechanic with procedural audio pitch sweeps.
   - Rune ignition sequence, energy conduit rotation, and expanding shockwave ring.
   - Custom GLSL simplex noise vortex shader with chromatic dispersion and event horizon depth.

3. **Scene 3: Interdimensional Travel & Warp Tunnel**
   - Seamless camera acceleration directly through the portal ring into the alien dimensions.
   - High-speed light streak warp tunnel.

4. **Scene 4: Four Distinct Dimensions**
   - **Dimension A: The Enchanted Sylva** (Emerald bioluminescent mushrooms, towering ancient canopy trees, cascading waterfalls, and glowing fireflies).
   - **Dimension B: Neo-Kowloon 2099** (Reflective rain-slick asphalt, procedural skyscrapers with neon billboards, and flying hovercars).
   - **Dimension C: Nebula Genesis** (Orbiting gas giants with rings, volumetric starfield, and asteroid belt).
   - **Dimension D: Abyssal Atlantis** (Sunken ruins, swimming schools of fish, and pulsing bioluminescent jellyfish).

5. **📱 9:16 Short-Form Recording Mode (Reels & Shorts)**
   - Dedicated portrait mode toggle (`9:16`) framed within mobile safe zones.
   - Clean UI mode for capturing screen recordings without clutter.

6. **🎬 Deterministic 25-Second Showcase Storyboard**
   - Fully automated cinematic flight showcasing all 4 dimensions in 25 seconds for consistent video production.

7. **🎵 Zero-Asset Procedural Audio Engine**
   - Synthesizes ambient sub-drones, charging hums, chime tones, and vortex whooshes using the Web Audio API.
